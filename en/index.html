FAQ

Export encoding, pseudo-tables, free limits — the things people ask most

How do I export a web page table to Excel?

Install the extension, open any page with a table, click the Table Export toolbar icon, and export to Excel (.xlsx), CSV, Markdown, or JSON in one click.

Can it export lists that aren't <table> elements?

Yes. Div-based repeated blocks such as product listings, rankings, and dashboards are detected as pseudo-tables and exported the same way.

Does it work offline? Is my data uploaded anywhere?

Fully offline. All detection and export happen locally in your browser. The extension makes zero network requests, and even Pro activation is verified offline.

What are the free tier limits?

Single-page export is free forever with no row limit. Batch paged collection: 500 rows/day and 200 rows per run. Pro ($9.99 one-time) unlocks unlimited paging and 50,000 rows per run.

Why does my CSV look garbled in Excel?

Exports include a UTF-8 BOM and open cleanly by double-clicking in Excel or WPS. If you use Excel's data-import wizard, select UTF-8 encoding manually.
