v0.2.5 · 2026-09-03 (current)

  • Detects standard <table> elements and div-based pseudo-tables (product listings, rankings, dashboard views);
  • One-click export to CSV (UTF-8 with BOM — no mojibake in Excel/WPS), Excel .xlsx, Markdown, and JSON;
  • rowspan / colspan merged cells are flattened into clean 2D data — rowspan values fill down so group info is preserved, ready for pivot tables;
  • Cross-table merge export (Pro): combine several tables on one page vertically or horizontally into a single file;
  • Batch paged collection with automatic next-page clicking; full-page-navigation pagination resumes across navigations;
  • Batch paging now runs in its own view with progress you can actually read: rows collected, current page, percentage, a four-step indicator and time remaining, plus "stop and export what I have". Leave and come back and the running task picks up where it was;
  • Floating in-page toolbar (master switch + per-button visibility), with pinnable preview before export;
  • Toolbar buttons carry format letter badges (download XLSX/CSV/MD/JSON, copy TXT/CSV/JSON) so they stay tellable at small sizes — and screen readers announce them too;
  • When a page can't be read, you get a next step that actually helps: browser-internal and store pages explain why and offer diagnostic info plus a FAQ link, unauthorised local files point at the exact permission to enable, and a page loaded before the extension gets a one-click reload;
  • Settings reordered by how often you use it: licence status sits on top (activated codes copy in full with one click), deactivation moved to a danger zone at the bottom behind a confirm step, so free users aren't met by a wall of purchase controls;
  • The copy menu is no longer clipped by card edges — near the viewport edge it pulls in or flips upward;
  • Button and body colours recalculated to WCAG AA; error states don't rely on colour alone;
  • Free tier: single-page export is free forever with no row limit; batch paging adds 500 rows/day (counted from page 2 onward), 200 rows per batch run;
  • Pro lifetime license $9.99: unlimited paging, up to 50,000 rows per batch run, offline activation codes;
  • The upgrade link goes straight to the pricing page — Chinese UI to the Chinese page, every other language to the English one;
  • 10 UI languages: Simplified & Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian; the post-install welcome page and toolbar tooltip follow your browser language;
  • Dark / light theme, follows system by default;
  • Fully offline, zero network requests, collects no data.

Updates are published to the Chrome Web Store and Microsoft Edge Add-ons; store installs update automatically.
